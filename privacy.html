Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

What Hana Drive accesses

Hana Drive signs in to one Google account that belongs to the owner's assistant. That account only holds the Google Drive folder the owner has shared with it. Hana Drive reads, creates, and moves files inside that folder. It does not use Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Files are copied to a private server controlled by the owner, processed there for the owner's own work, and results are written back to the same folder. Content may be processed by AI services the owner uses under the owner's own accounts.

Hana Drive does not sell data, does not share it for advertising, and does not give it to anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

The owner can stop access at any time by removing the folder share or by revoking Hana Drive in the Google account's security settings. The access token is then deleted from the server.
